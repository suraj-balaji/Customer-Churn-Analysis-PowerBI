# Business Problem

## Overview

Customer churn is one of the most critical business challenges faced by telecom companies. High customer churn directly impacts revenue, profitability, customer acquisition costs, and long-term business growth.

Databel Telecom experienced increasing customer churn rates and required a business intelligence solution capable of identifying the primary factors influencing customer attrition.

The company wanted to better understand:
- Why customers are leaving
- Which customer groups are at highest risk
- Which business factors influence churn behavior
- How pricing, contracts, and service quality impact retention
- What retention strategies could reduce customer loss

---

## Business Challenges

The organization faced several key business challenges:

### 1. High Customer Churn
A significant percentage of customers were leaving the service, creating revenue instability and increased acquisition costs.

### 2. Limited Visibility into Churn Drivers
The company lacked centralized visibility into the major causes of churn including:
- competitor pressure,
- pricing dissatisfaction,
- service quality concerns,
- and customer support issues.

### 3. Contract Retention Problems
Short-term contract customers demonstrated unstable retention behavior, but the business lacked detailed analytical insight into contract-related churn risk.

### 4. Customer Support Dissatisfaction
Frequent customer service interactions appeared connected to churn behavior, but operational insights were limited.

### 5. Pricing & Usage Mismatch
The company suspected that certain customers were subscribed to plans that did not align with their actual usage behavior.

### 6. Demographic Risk Patterns
The organization needed better understanding of churn behavior across different customer demographics and age groups.

---

## Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this project were:

- Analyze customer churn behavior using Power BI
- Identify high-risk customer segments
- Investigate churn drivers across contracts, demographics, usage, and support interactions
- Build executive-level dashboards for decision-making
- Create interactive business intelligence reporting
- Generate actionable business recommendations
- Improve analytical visibility into retention performance

---

## Business Questions Addressed

This analysis aimed to answer several key business questions:

- Which customer segments demonstrate the highest churn rates?
- Which contract types are most associated with churn?
- How do customer service calls influence churn behavior?
- Does unlimited plan usage impact customer retention?
- Which churn reasons occur most frequently?
- Which geographic regions show elevated churn risk?
- How does account tenure affect retention?
- What actions could improve customer retention?

---

## Expected Business Value

The project was designed to help the business:
- reduce customer churn,
- improve retention strategies,
- optimize pricing structures,
- improve customer support performance,
- identify high-risk customers earlier,
- and support data-driven decision-making.

The final dashboard solution provides an interactive analytical framework for monitoring customer churn behavior and supporting strategic retention planning.
